2. Guide by the Information Regulator

A guide on how to use PAIA is available from the Information Regulator (South Africa): inforegulator.org.za.

3. Records available without a PAIA request

The following categories of records are generally available on request without a formal PAIA application:

  • Company registration details (CIPC).
  • This website's public content.
  • Marketing material.

4. Records held (subject to PAIA request)

Records held by the company include, where applicable: client contracts, project documentation, financial records, employee records, supplier agreements, and correspondence.

5. How to request access

Requests must be made on Form 2 of the PAIA regulations, addressed to the Information Officer, at hello@silverbyte.co.za. A request fee and access fee may apply as prescribed by regulation.

6. Grounds for refusal

Access may be refused on grounds set out in Chapter 4 of PAIA, including where the record contains personal information of a third party, commercial information of a third party, or information that would prejudice a legitimate investigation.

7. Remedies

If a request is refused, the requester may lodge a complaint with the Information Regulator or apply to a court for relief under section 78 of PAIA.

8. Availability

This manual is available on this page and on request by email.
