What ships

Every build ships with the automation that runs it.

Not a product here and a retainer there. One bundle: the software, the AI and integrations that keep it moving, and a support line that answers.

Worked example

A store that reorders stock and answers its own support email.

We built Northwind's storefront on a headless commerce stack. When SKU inventory drops below the 14-day velocity threshold, a workflow drafts a purchase order, flags it for one-click approval, and logs it in their ERP.

Routine support email — order status, returns, sizing — is triaged by an AI assistant grounded in their catalog and policy docs, and handed to a human only when confidence drops below 0.82.

export const reorder = defineFlow({ trigger: 'inventory.sku.threshold', when: (sku) => sku.daysOfCover() < 14, steps: [ draftPurchaseOrder({ supplier: 'auto' }), flagForApproval({ channel: 'slack' }), syncToERP({ ledger: 'northwind' }), ], // runs on every stock change, no cron });
