Enquiry emails: up to 24 months after the last contact. Server logs: 30 days.

7. Your rights under POPIA

You have the right to:

To exercise any of these, email hello@silverbyte.co.za. We respond within 30 days.

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect personal information, including HTTPS on this site and access controls on our email and hosting accounts. No system is perfectly secure; we cannot guarantee absolute security.
